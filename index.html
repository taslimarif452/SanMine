What is SanMine Space?

SanMine Space is an AI-powered workspace designed to help users research topics, work with information, develop proposals, and prepare professional outreach from one place.

  • AI Research: Research and explore information with AI assistance.
  • Workspace: Organize conversations, ideas, and research in one place.
  • Proposal Creation: Turn research and conversations into structured professional proposals.
  • Gmail Outreach: Connect a Google account and send outreach emails through Gmail with the user's authorization.
